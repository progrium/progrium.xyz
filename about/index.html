about

I'm Jeff Lindsay. Online I go by progrium. I've spent about twenty years building developer tools and open source infrastructure, usually small things meant to be composed into bigger ones.

Some of it you may have used: webhooks, localtunnel, Dokku. Since 2020 most of my work has gone into Tractor, a search for simpler ways to build software. Wanix, Apptron and h9 all come out of that.

Otherwise I make indie games and music. Someday I'd like to make films.
