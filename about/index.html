about

I'm Jeff Lindsay. I've spent most of my life dedicated to building better developer tools and open source infrastructure, usually small building blocks meant to be composed into bigger ones.

Some of them you may have used: webhooks, Docker, Twilio, Dokku. Since 2020 most of my work has gone into Tractor, searching for a few simple primitives to build any software. Wanix, Apptron, and h9 all come out of that.

Otherwise I make indie games and music when I can. I also used to build communities. Someday I'd like to make films.
